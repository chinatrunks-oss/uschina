01 / THE STORY

The numbers tell the story

数字即叙事

Most meme coins pick a supply at random. USCHINA's numbers were chosen one by one — each one points at a date, a treaty, or an economy.

Total Supply · 总量
52,000,000,000,000

52 trillion tokens. The combined nominal GDP of China and the US is roughly $52.3 trillion — rounded down to 52 trillion, and minted once, in full, on October 2, 2026. There will never be more.

52 万亿枚，对应中美名义 GDP 之和约 52.3 万亿美元，取整。2026 年 10 月 2 日一次铸完，永不增发。

The Pool Opens · 建池
1844

On October 10, 2026, the USCHINA/WETH pool opened on Aerodrome with 1.844 trillion USCHINA + 0.001844 WETH — a nod to 1844, the year of the Treaty of Wanghia, the first treaty between China and the United States.

2026 年 10 月 10 日在 Aerodrome 建池：1.844 万亿枚 + 0.001844 WETH，致敬 1844 年中美第一份条约《望厦条约》。

Signing Day · 签约日
7 · 03

The treaty was signed on July 3, 1844. On October 22, 2026, the pool was deepened by exactly 7.03 trillion USCHINA + 0.00703 ETH — the "703 top-up" — so the signing date lives inside the pool's balance.

《望厦条约》签于 1844 年 7 月 3 日。2026 年 10 月 22 日按「703」加深池子：7.03 万亿枚 + 0.00703 ETH。

The Relay · 接龙
34 · 5 · 12

The treaty had 34 articles, opened five ports to trade, and allowed revision after 12 years. Those three numbers — 34, 5, 12 — are reserved as the amounts for future pool top-ups, in that order.

条约共 34 款、五口通商、12 年后可修约。这三个数字已定为后续加深池子的接龙数额，依次进行。
